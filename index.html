The rules of minesweeper are simple: avoid the slaps from Marshall Eriksen! ✋🏻✋🏻✋🏻

  • Select a tile to reveal what's hidden underneath. Your first pick is always safe.
  • A numbered tile indicates the number of slaps in the tile's proximity. (Slaps are approved by Lily, the Slap Bet commissioner.)
  • If you know a tile has a slap, you can mark it with a flag 🚩 by double clicking or right clicking on it (or long pressing on a touch screen).
  • Avoid the slaps and reveal all the safe tiles to win!

Suit up!
